Rules & payouts

This is an independent 120xBet free-coin game. The rules below are for this version. Coins have no cash or prize value and cannot be purchased or withdrawn.

Each paid spin deducts the selected bet once. The server draws three numbers and one bonus. Winning payouts include any return of the stake and are credited automatically.

ResultPayout
Three matching nonzero numbersBet × matching number
Exactly two matching nonzero numbers, any positionsBet × number × 0.2
No nonzero match / three zeros0
2x / 5x / 10xMultiply the matching-number payout; 10x requires bet ≥ 10
ALL WINBet × sum of all three numbers × 0.1
SCATTERWheel prize replaces the number payout; wheel values scale with the bet
ALL RESPINThree extra number-only spins, no additional bet; all payouts added

Number weights per reel: 0 = 55%, 1 = 28%, 5 = 14%, 10 = 3%. Bonus weights: none 70%, 2x 12%, 5x 6%, 10x 2%, Scatter 3%, All Win 4%, All Respin 3%. At bet 5, the 10x weight becomes 2x.

At bet 5, wheel prize weights are: 25 (30%), 50 (25%), 75 (18%), 100 (12%), 150 (8%), 250 (4%), 500 (2%), 1000 (1%). Higher displayed prizes are less likely. The wheel is a bonus only when Scatter appears; its position on a regular spin does not award a prize.

Payouts use two decimal places. Auto Play stops when its count finishes, balance is insufficient, the page is hidden, or you press Stop. Turbo changes animation speed only.
